Frequently Asked Questions

Is FirstStep Careers completely free for freshers?

Yes, 100%. FirstStep Careers never charges candidates for registering, uploading resumes, or applying to jobs. Beware of any recruiter asking for money; report them immediately.

What resume file formats are accepted?

You can upload standard document formats including PDF (.pdf), Microsoft Word (.doc, .docx) up to 5MB in size.

How do recruiters review applications?

Recruiters have a dedicated Applicants dashboard where they can review your profile, download your resume, update your application status, and leave interview feedback.

How does company verification work?

Our platform administrators review all registered company accounts to verify official business email domains and legitimate physical locations before awarding the verified badge.
